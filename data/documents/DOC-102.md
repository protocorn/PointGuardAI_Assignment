# Intern Onboarding Checklist

- Document ID: DOC-102
- Clearance: junior
- Summary: First-week steps for interns and junior hires, including accounts, mentor, and training.

Welcome to Harborline Systems. This checklist is for interns and junior employees in their first ten business days.

Day 1: collect your badge from the lobby, join the Harborline-Guest network, and open the starter checklist in the team notebook. Your manager confirms your laptop login before lunch.

Day 2: meet your mentor for thirty minutes. The mentor is another engineer on the same team, not a director. Write down the name of your team channel and the time of the daily standup.

Days 3 to 5: finish the workplace-conduct course and the data-handling course marked "junior." Both courses are short and have a quiz. You do not take the senior customer-notes course or the management compensation course.

By day 10: ship one small documentation fix or a labeled starter task. Post the link in the team channel. Your mentor, not HR, reviews it.

Questions about this checklist go to your mentor or to People Operations through the public intern channel. Pay, equity, and acquisition topics are outside this document.
